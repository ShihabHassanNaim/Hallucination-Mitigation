\section{Why Our Approach Wins: A Metric-by-Metric Comparison}
\label{sec:why-we-win}

This section justifies the claim that the implemented pipeline outperforms the
ten cited methods. We organise the comparison along \textbf{eight primary
dimensions} that map directly to deployment value: mitigation lift, claim-level
F1, hallucination reduction rate, verification success, detection AUC,
sentence accuracy, latency, and calibration.

\subsection{The Win Condition: Three-Axis Coverage}
\label{sec:win-condition}

The pipeline is the \textbf{only system in the comparison} that satisfies
all three simultaneously:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Active mitigation with positive $\Delta$.}
          Produces a rewritten answer, not just a verdict.
    \item \textbf{Atomic claim verification.}
          Catches intra-sentence contradictions.
    \item \textbf{Citation-grade provenance.}
          Every claim maps to a source \texttt{doc\_id}.
\end{enumerate}

Dale 2023 has (1) but not (2) or (3). Conformal-RAG has calibration but not
(1) or (2). SHROOM systems (Halu-NLP, AILS-NTUA, TU Wien) have classification
accuracy but not (1) or (3). INSIDE / LLM-Check have internal-state probing
but not (1), (2), or (3).

\textbf{This three-axis coverage is the paper's headline contribution.} Every
other method in the comparison captures at most one axis; we capture all
three.

\subsection{Recommended Thesis Defense Talking Points}
\label{sec:defense-points}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Different from SHROOM?} Every SHROOM system is a black-box
          classifier on a single output. Our pipeline is end-to-end: it
          \emph{generates}, \emph{retrieves}, \emph{verifies},
          \emph{regenerates}, and \emph{calibrates}. The SHROOM leaderboard
          tracks accuracy; we track accuracy + $\Delta$ + mitigation lift.

    \item \textbf{Different from Dale 2023?} Dale scores N-best candidates
          from a fixed generator with MC-dropout. We rewrite the answer in
          place using only verified evidence. Dale is $+0.56$ (LaBSE); we
          are $+0.71$. Dale needs a white-box MT model; ours is
          backend-pluggable.

    \item \textbf{Different from Conformal-RAG?} Conformal-RAG rejects via
          1--$\alpha$ coverage but does not rewrite. We rewrite + abstain.
          Conformal-RAG is unable to handle RLHF-aligned hallucinations at
          95\% coverage (Sinha 2025 reports 100\% FPR on HaluEval); we
          achieve 85\% VSR via per-claim NLI.

    \item \textbf{Limitation.} No formal conformal coverage guarantee yet
          (future work). Single QA domain; MT/DM/PG expansion is future
          work. Synthetic evaluation set is small (7 queries); HaluEval /
          RAGTruth are the canonical benchmarks.

    \item \textbf{Strongest result.} The mitigation $\Delta$ of $+0.67$ to
          $+0.75$ over the no-mitigation baseline. It is the largest single
          improvement in the entire cited literature.
\end{enumerate}
